A split leaves two half-full nodes behind. Whether they fill up again depends on where the next keys land. Insert the same keys in ascending order and in random order, and compare how full the nodes end up.

This is the second page of Chapter 04: Insertion and splitting; the first one, Insert a key, shows a single insert step by step. Here two trees of order M receive the same N keys (0 to N − 1), one tree in ascending order and one in a shuffled order. Fill means: keys stored, divided by the key slots of all nodes. The shuffle is the SplitMix64 shuffle of the chapter's programs, so seed 4 gives the same sequence as their output.
