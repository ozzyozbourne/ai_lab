M3: one transformer decode step

The Gantt view runs left to right in clock cycles: each upper row is one instruction queue (grouped by slice, West to East), with a block at the cycle an instruction was issued, labelled with its opcode, and a thin whisker on to the cycle its result becomes visible. Each lower row is one stream lane, where E n is eastward-flowing stream n and W n is westward-flowing stream n, and a bar starts in the cycle a value is driven onto that stream.

The solid part of a bar lasts until the last instruction that reads the value; the faded, hatched part (the dead tail) is the value still travelling, one position per cycle, until another value overwrites it or it leaves the chip. Colour identifies a value (its tag without the byte-plane suffix #j) and repeats in large programs, so hover any block, bar or cell to highlight that one value everywhere and read its tag. The chip view below shows every stream cell at the selected cycle, which you pick with the slider, the arrow keys, or by clicking the Gantt.
