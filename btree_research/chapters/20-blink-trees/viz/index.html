A reader is on its way to a leaf when a writer splits that leaf. You decide which thread takes the next step. Watch the high key and the dashed right link save the reader, then switch right links off and watch the same key go missing.

What to look for

  • One latch at a time. After every reader step the reader holds nothing, only the number of the node it wants next. That gap is where a split can happen. The S and X badges show who holds which latch right now.
  • The high key detects the split. The dashed cell at the right end of a node is its high key: every key in the node is below it. A search key at or above the high key means "my keys moved": follow the dashed right link.
  • A split is two steps. After step 1 the new node hangs beside its sibling with no line from the parent. Step 2 draws that line (the downlink) and clears the flag. Between the two steps the tree is already correct.
  • Writer dies after step 1. Choose the second scenario: the flag stays, readers still find everything by moving right, and the next writer that touches the node finishes the split before its own work.
  • Without right links. Untick Reader follows right links and replay. The reader lands on the node it was sent to, does not find the key, and reports "not found" for a key that is in the tree.
