A delete does not remove anything from the index: it only writes a number into a row version. Watch dead entries pile up, see who is allowed to remove them and when, and follow an empty page through its two-phase deletion until it is safe to reuse.

What to look for

  • An entry reads key:xmin-xmax. The two numbers live in the version store on the right, not in the index. 20:2-4 is the version of row 20 created by transaction 2 and ended by transaction 4. A snapshot is one number; it sees the versions with xmin ≤ snapshot and no xmax ≤ snapshot.
  • The horizon. It is the oldest open snapshot (or now). A version with xmax ≤ horizon is dead to everyone. Open a snapshot, delete some rows and run Vacuum: nothing is removed until the snapshot is closed.
  • Hints. Play scenario 1. A scan that meets a dead version sets ! on its index entry. The next insert that finds that leaf full drops the hinted entries instead of splitting.
  • Half-dead, deleted, free: follow one box. Play scenario 2 with Predict ticked and watch page #1. Phase 1: the edge from its parent disappears, but the box stays in the leaf row, between its neighbours, dashed (half-dead). Phase 2: the chain arrow of its left neighbour now passes over it and the box drops into the row of deleted pages with a stamp; the dashed red arrow is the right link it keeps. Only when the horizon reaches the stamp does it drop into the free list, and the next split lifts it back into the tree. The scan reads next pill stays on the box the whole time: that is the handle the paused scan holds.
  • Break it. Play scenario 3, or do it yourself: press Empty, put nine keys (10, 20, … 90), delete four neighbours in the middle (30, 40, 50, 60), and only then start a scan and read one page. Run Vacuum (a page is deleted but must wait: the scan's snapshot is older than its stamp), press Recycle anyway, put 81, 82, … until a split reuses the page, then let the scan read on. The order matters: rows deleted after the scan started are still visible to it, so Vacuum would not remove them.
  • Clicking fast is fine. If you press a control while the previous action is still being played, that action is run to its end first and the caption says so. If you stepped back yourself, the control continues from the step on screen.
