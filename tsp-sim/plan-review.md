# Plan review — one round

One independent reviewer (Opus) read only `plan.md` and `research/dossier.md`
and returned 20 numbered findings. This file records what was done with each.
There was no second review round.

"Accepted" means the plan, the ledger or a contract file was changed as
described. Nothing was rejected outright; four items were accepted in part, and
the part not taken is explained.

| # | Severity | Finding | Decision | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BLOCKER | M3 shapes do not type-check: `Kc` 8×16 and `SUM0` 1×16 give vectors of length 8 and 1 that cannot be added to length-16 vectors; `matvec_cols` with 8 columns against a 16-element operand is undefined | Accepted | `Kc` and `SUM0` are now 16×16 with zero rows (plan §6). `matvec_cols` is defined for `len(cols) ≤ len(x)` and uses `x[0..k−1]` (plan §3.3, `tsp/graph.py`) |
| 2 | BLOCKER | Two memory operands of one operation parked in the same slices need two Reads from one slice in one cycle; that holds for every anchor, so the search never ends. Cases: `e * (r·r)`, `h + f` | Accepted, both suggested fixes | Chain rule 2 now joins the first *qualifying* computed operand, not only the first operand (plan §5.4). New **co-read rule**: the slices of two memory-sourced operands of one operation must be disjoint; enforced in placement for leaves and in the parking-slice choice for intermediates; an unavoidable violation is an immediate `CompileError` (plan §5.2, §5.6). The false claim "a later anchor is always feasible" is replaced by the precise condition |
| 3 | BLOCKER | Weight installs of computed rows (`c16(v)` in `matvec_cols`) are not ordered after the Write that parks them | Accepted | Producers of weight rows are chain predecessors (plan §5.4); install Reads sample after the row's Write plus `d_func(WRITE)` (plan §5.5, §5.6 step 1) |
| 4 | MAJOR | The install search can loop: unspecified grouping may put two planes of one slice in one wave; no bound on step 1 | Accepted | Waves are packed greedily with no two planes per slice; each wave search is bounded by `max_schedule_cycles` (plan §5.6 step 1) |
| 5 | MAJOR | `r * r`: is `r` one consumer or two, and how is the second operand obtained | Accepted | Consumers are counted by node; both operand slots may name the same stream group (plan §2.7, §5.4) |
| 6 | MAJOR | Simulator and reference will disagree in the last bit; with F16 casts in the path that grows to ~1e-4, beyond the stated tolerance | Accepted | New lead-owned `tsp/numerics.py` defines every arithmetic kernel once; the F16 matvec uses exact products and `math.fsum`. Multi-tile matvec is defined as the F32 sum of per-tile products (plan §3.3). The bit-exact reference uses these kernels and must match exactly. A second, independent plain-NumPy reference (`evaluate_plain`) shares no code and is compared with a stated loose tolerance, so the shared kernels themselves are still cross-checked (plan §4, §9) |
| 7 | MAJOR | Cross-machine and cross-process non-determinism the tests cannot see (SIMD `exp`, BLAS, NaN payloads, byte order, RNG streams, hash seed) | Accepted in part | Done: explicit little-endian dtypes; NaN canonicalisation; no BLAS or SIMD math in float kernels; model data from a hand-written LCG; determinism tests run in two processes with different `PYTHONHASHSEED`; golden digests for M1 and M2. **Not done:** a golden digest for M3 and a cross-platform bit guarantee for `EXP`/`TANH`. They go through the C library's `exp`/`tanh`, which is not guaranteed correctly rounded everywhere; writing our own correctly rounded `exp` is out of proportion for this project. The limitation is stated in plan §2.7 and §9 and will be in the README. NumPy is not pinned, because no result depends on NumPy's distribution or math routines any more |
| 8 | MAJOR | The fp16 "tandem" rule has no ledger entry and reinterprets "two byte-planes" as "both MXM planes" | Accepted | New ledger row L-X9 (INFERRED capacity from "two FP16 or four int8 sub-operations each cycle", ASSUMED mechanism), cited in plan §2.8 and §3.3. | 9 | MAJOR | Untraceable numbers: no `d_func` for WRITE/IW; "issued later" vs "sampled later"; `max_schedule_cycles` has no ledger value; issue widths and SXM unit list outside `ChipConfig`; `planes_per_mxm` ambiguous | Accepted | L-T4 and L-T7 are now `d_func` values with a sample-cycle rule (ledger, plan §2.3). | 10 | MAJOR | Contract holes: `MemInit`, `Report`, `Memory.read` type, Graph read API, paper-size M1 | Accepted | All defined (plan §3.3, §3.4, §4); `models.m1(n_in, n_out)` |
