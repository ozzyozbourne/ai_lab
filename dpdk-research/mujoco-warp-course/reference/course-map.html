    The Course Map

    Thirty chapters in six Parts, from "what is a physics simulator" to GPU-native reinforcement learning. This page shows how they depend on each other, which chapter owns which idea, and where each stage of mjw.step is taught. Pick a chapter to see what you need before it and what it unlocks.

    • Who owns which concept, every reference page, and a 20-question quiz that mixes all six Parts.

    2Reading paths to a goal

    You do not have to read in order. Pick a goal and the graph selects it: the blue chapters are the shortest complete path (every hard prerequisite, transitively), listed in reading order in the details panel above.

    Two short tracks

    For RL only: Ch 1-10, then 24, 26, 28 §1-§3 and 30 (the capstone's prerequisites). For physics internals: Ch 1-7, then 11-14 and 19-20. Part IV (collision) can wait until your scenes need meshes, terrain or many contacts.

    6Cumulative quiz

    Twenty questions across all six Parts. Each answer links to the section that owns it. Each Part's last chapter also ends with its own Part review.

    Course quiz

    Part IWhich call copies a host MjModel into device arrays (and refuses unsupported features)?

    put_model inspects, converts to float32 and adds warp-only fields. Ch 5 §2

    Part IIn what order does MJWarp store a quaternion?

    MuJoCo quats are (w, x, y, z), on host and device. Ch 2 §3, Ch 4 §6

    Part IWarp sees no GPU. What happens when you call mjw.step?

    Warp silently falls back to CPU; the setup cell prints the device. Ch 4 §1

    Part IA batched Model field has leading size 1 and nworld = 8. Which row does world 5 read?

    Kernels index batched Model fields with worldid % shape[0]. Ch 6 §7

    Part IInside mjw.forward, where is the mass matrix factorized?

    _forward pairs fwd_position(factorize=False) with fwd_acceleration(factorize=True). Ch 11 §9

    Part IIWhen is a bit in d.overflow cleared?

    Nothing clears a bit except reset_data. Ch 9 §7

    Part IIWhat does nconmax guarantee?

    "One world may have more than nconmax contacts." Ch 9 §5

    Part IIYou give every world a different body_mass. What else must you do?

    Ch 10 §6 and Ch 10 §7

    Part IIIHow is d.M stored?

    Use M_rowadr / M_rownnz / M_colind, or mjw.mul_m. Ch 11 §8

    Part IIIWith the RK4 integrator, how often does the control callback run per mjw.step?

    Stages 2-4 see intermediate states. Ch 13 §4

    Part IVYou edit m.geom_contype after put_model. Which rigid pairs collide now?

    Edit the MjModel and call put_model again. Ch 15 §3

    Part IVWhat does EPA add to GJK?

    Ch 17 §5

    Part VThe Newton direction is -H^{-1}g. What is H?

    Ch 20 §6; background in the math toolkit.

    Part VWhat does the exact linesearch minimize?

    Ch 20 §8; math toolkit.

    Part VA reproducibility test passes on CPU. What does it prove about the GPU?

    Ch 22 §1

    Part VIAfter capturing a graph you set m.opt.iterations = 2. What does the replay do?

    Ch 24 §4

    Part VIAnd after m.opt.timestep.fill_(0.002)?

    Ch 24 §4

    Part IWhich flags record segmentation with mjwarp-record?

    Ch 5 §9; troubleshooting.

    Part VIYou keep t = wp.to_torch(d.qpos) across steps. What do you get?

    Clone what you keep. Ch 28 §1

    Part VIWhy does Ch 29 identify parameters with finite differences and CEM instead of a wp.Tape through mjw.step?
